** RACE GROUPS - read by Extended Boostables (your.mod.racegroup.RaceGroups).
**
** Each entry declares a group of races that acts as ONE pseudo-race in the POPULATION_* requirement
** values used by REQUIRES blocks (techs, events, room unlocks, titles). For a group key <G> you get:
**   POPULATION_<G>__F, POPULATION_<G>__I
**   POPULATION_<G>_<CLASS>_F, POPULATION_<G>_<CLASS>_I, POPULATION_<G>_<CLASS>_OFCLASS_F
** with <CLASS> = NOBLE, CITIZEN or SLAVE. Example: POPULATION_GAQUATIC_CITIZEN_OFCLASS_F: 1 in a
** GREATERE block = every citizen is one of the group's races, in any mix.
**
** Any mod can ship its own V71/RaceGroups.txt. Files from all loaded mods are merged: a group's
** RACES are combined across files, and NAME comes from the highest-priority mod that sets it (the
** same mod whose files win when two mods ship the same file). Races that
** are not loaded are skipped, so the keys always exist even if some member race mods are missing.
**
** Convention: prefix group keys with G ("group") so they can't collide with a race key.
** NAME is the in-game label; the loaded member races are listed after it automatically.

GAQUATIC: {
	NAME: "Aquatic",
	RACES: [VERATHID, KHELIAN, THRYXARII, ONARI,],
},
